Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: 6 October 2026

1. About this policy

ZERO SHOT LAB develops Rexo Rush: Neon Reflex Tap (Android package com.rexo.rush). This policy explains how the current offline Android game, its advertising services, and this policy website handle information. Contact: randhir2709vns@gmail.com.

2. Game information stored on your device

Your chosen player name, local profile identifier, scores, coins, skins, power-ups, achievements, level progress, streaks and settings are saved on your device. The current version does not use a developer-operated game server, password-based registration, cloud sync or an online leaderboard. The leaderboard is simulated locally. We do not receive your local game save through the game.

3. Advertising and third-party data

The Android app uses Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising/app-set and applicable device or account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google and its advertising partners process this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising may be used depending on device settings and applicable consent requirements. We do not directly collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings for gameplay. Advertising partners process data under their own policies; see Google’s Privacy Policy and advertising controls linked below.

4. Permissions, reminders and sharing

Optional local notifications remind you about streaks. You can disable them through Android notification settings. Gameplay uses sound playback and vibration. If you use the share feature, the content you choose is passed to the app or service you select; its privacy policy applies.

5. Purchases and virtual items

The current release has no real-money in-app purchases or active RevenueCat integration. Coins and virtual items are earned through gameplay or rewarded ads. If purchases are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the payment platform, purchase records and any purchase-management provider before that release.

6. Information you send us

If you email ZERO SHOT LAB, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to support or privacy inquiries. Please avoid sending passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. Support information is retained while needed to resolve your request, handle follow-up or meet applicable legal requirements; you may request deletion by email.

7. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you delete it. In the app, open Home → Settings → Delete Account to remove the local game profile and progress. To remove remaining local preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Rexo Rush → Storage → Clear storage/data. This resets progress and cannot be undone. We cannot restore a local save. Device backups may retain copies under your operating system or backup provider’s controls. Clearing the game’s data does not erase information already processed by Google or advertising partners; their retention and deletion practices apply. Use Google’s privacy controls or contact us for assistance with a privacy request.

8. Your choices and data protection

Android and Google settings provide advertising identifier and ad-personalization controls; availability depends on your Android version. Local notification permission is optional. You may contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us and other applicable privacy rights. Local storage relies on your device’s protections. Google reports that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Children

Rexo Rush is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete information we control. A release intended for children would require corresponding advertising and policy changes.

10. This website and external links

These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. ZERO SHOT LAB has not added cookies, advertising or analytics scripts to these pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. External websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

11. Changes and contact

Effective and last updated: 6 October 2026. Changes will be published here with an updated date. For privacy inquiries, contact ZERO SHOT LAB at randhir2709vns@gmail.com.
