Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: 16 October 2026

1. About this policy

ZERO SHOT LAB develops Rexo Rush: Neon Reflex Tap (Android package com.rexo.rush). This policy explains how the Android game, optional Google Play Games cloud saves, purchase and advertising services, and this policy website handle information. Contact: randhir2709vns@gmail.com.

2. Game information stored on your device

Your chosen player name, local profile identifier, scores, coins, skins, power-ups, achievements, level progress, streaks and settings are saved on your device. You can play with a guest profile without connecting Google Play Games. The game has no developer-operated global leaderboard; personal bests are saved locally.

3. Optional Google Play Games and cloud saves

If you connect Google Play Games, the game receives your Play Games Player ID and display name, rather than your Google email address or password. Google Saved Games stores a copy of your game progress, including coins, items, scores and level records, so you can restore it with the same Play Games account. On first connection, you choose phone or cloud progress before auto-save starts. You can disconnect cloud saving from Play Games & Cloud Save in the game. Conflicting saves require a choice and are not added together. Google processes the connection and saves under its own policies.

4. Advertising and third-party data

The Android app uses Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising/app-set and applicable device or account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google and its advertising partners process this information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising may be used depending on device settings and applicable consent requirements. We do not directly collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings for gameplay. Advertising partners process data under their own policies; see Google’s Privacy Policy and advertising controls linked below.

5. Permissions, reminders and sharing

Daily streak reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device for 10:00 AM local time after you enable them and grant notification permission. You can turn them off in Home → Settings or from the Streak page, or disable notifications in Android settings. A test notification is available in Settings. Reminder status and review-prompt timing are kept on your device; this reminder feature does not register a remote push token or send your schedule to a developer server. Gameplay uses sound playback and vibration. If you use the share feature, the content you choose is passed to the app or service you select; its privacy policy applies.

6. Purchases and virtual items

The Android game uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for a one-time lifetime ad-free purchase. Google handles payment; ZERO SHOT LAB does not receive your card details. RevenueCat is initialized when the Android app starts to check purchase access. It processes an app user identifier, purchase history, purchase tokens and transaction records, plus app/device information used to validate, manage and restore access. This integration does not send your chosen player name, Google email address or Play Games Player ID to RevenueCat as customer attributes. An active ad-free entitlement blocks every in-game ad, including rewarded videos. Coins and virtual items are earned through gameplay, daily rewards and, for players without ad-free access, optional rewarded ads. There are no real-money coin packs in this version.

7. Information you send us

If you email ZERO SHOT LAB, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to support or privacy inquiries. Please avoid sending passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. Support information is retained while needed to resolve your request, handle follow-up or meet applicable legal requirements; you may request deletion by email.

8. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you delete it. In the app, open Home → Settings → Delete Account to remove the local game profile and progress. To remove remaining local preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Rexo Rush → Storage → Clear storage/data. This resets progress on that device. A previously synced Play Games cloud save can be restored after connecting the same Play Games account. Deleting local progress disconnects cloud saving but does not delete the Google cloud save or purchase records. Manage or delete Play Games data through your Google account/Play Games settings; Google and RevenueCat handle purchase record retention under their policies. Device backups may retain copies under your operating system or backup provider’s controls. Clearing the game’s data does not erase information already processed by Google or advertising partners; their retention and deletion practices apply. Use Google’s privacy controls or contact us for assistance with a privacy request. If you want help requesting deletion of purchase-management data, contact us at randhir2709vns@gmail.com. Records required for transactions, fraud prevention or legal obligations may need to be retained. Deleting data does not by itself request a refund or cancel a completed one-time purchase.

9. Your choices and data protection

Where required, Google’s consent message lets you make advertising privacy choices before ads are requested. When the option is available, open Home → Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change them. Android and Google settings also provide advertising identifier and ad-personalization controls; availability varies by device and region. Local notification permission and connecting Play Games are optional. Buying ad-free prevents future in-game ad requests while access is verified; it does not delete data previously processed by advertising services or stop purchase verification. You may email us to request access, correction or deletion of information we control, including assistance with RevenueCat customer records. We may need enough information to identify the relevant record. Local storage relies on your device’s protections. Google and RevenueCat state that their SDK data is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. Children

Rexo Rush is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete information we control. A release intended for children would require corresponding advertising and policy changes.

11. Ratings and reviews

Rating the game is optional. The game may request a Google Play review after you have played for a while, and a Rate & review option opens the Play Store. The game does not receive your selected stars or know whether you submitted a review. Google Play handles ratings and review text under its policies; reviews may be public, while feedback in test tracks may be private. Manage or delete your reviews through your Google account or Play Store controls. No in-game reward depends on a rating.

12. This website and external links

These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. ZERO SHOT LAB has not added cookies, advertising or analytics scripts to these pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. External websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

13. Changes and contact

Effective and last updated: 16 October 2026. Changes will be published here with an updated date. For privacy inquiries, contact ZERO SHOT LAB at randhir2709vns@gmail.com.
